Website Site Plan

Diego Granados | Web Developer Portfolio

Project Overview

Subject of the Website

A personal portfolio website for Diego Granados as a web developer, designed to showcase my skills, professional experience, and personal brand identity in the tech industry.

Planned Content

The website will consist of the following pages and sections:

  • Home Page: A welcoming introduction featuring a professional photograph, a concise summary of who I am, and a clear statement of what I do as a web developer.
  • About / Experience Page: A detailed overview of my work history, technical skill set, and a visual timeline of my career progression from student to professional developer.
  • Projects Page: A curated showcase of my best work, including project descriptions, the technologies and frameworks used, placeholder screenshots, and direct links to live demos or GitHub repositories.
  • Certificates Section: A comprehensive list of my professional certifications with brief descriptions explaining their relevance and links to verification pages where applicable.
  • Contact Page: A user-friendly contact form, my professional email address, and links to my LinkedIn profile, GitHub account, and other relevant professional networks.

Reason for Choosing This Subject

I selected a personal portfolio website because it serves a dual purpose in my web development journey. First, it provides a practical platform where I can apply and demonstrate my proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through real-world implementation. Second, it creates a professional asset that I can actively use when applying for jobs, pitching to freelance clients, and documenting my growth as a developer over time. Building my own portfolio allows me to control my professional narrative while continuously improving both the site and my skills.

Site Name

DiegoDev

The site name "DiegoDev" combines my first name with a clear indicator of my profession, creating a memorable and brandable identity. This naming approach accomplishes several goals:

  • It immediately communicates that the site belongs to a developer named Diego
  • The shortened "Dev" suffix is widely recognized in the tech community
  • It is concise enough to be easily remembered and typed into a browser
  • The personal name creates a human connection while maintaining professionalism
  • It works well as both a site title and a potential personal brand across social platforms

Optional domain idea: diegodev.com or diego-lara.dev

Site Purpose

The primary purpose of this portfolio website is to serve as a comprehensive digital representation of my capabilities, experience, and professional identity as a web developer. In today's competitive job market, having a well-crafted online presence is no longer optional—it is expected by employers and clients alike.

This website will accomplish the following objectives:

  • Showcase Technical Projects: Display my completed work with detailed descriptions of the problems solved, technologies implemented, and the development process used, giving visitors tangible evidence of my abilities.
  • Demonstrate Front-End Proficiency: The website itself serves as a live example of my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills. Visitors can evaluate my code quality, design sensibilities, and attention to detail simply by experiencing the site.
  • Present Professional Experience: Provide hiring managers and recruiters with a well-organized overview of my work history, technical skills, and career trajectory in a format more engaging than a traditional resume.
  • Establish Credibility: Display certifications, testimonials, and links to external profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn) that validate my qualifications and professional standing.
  • Facilitate Contact: Make it effortless for potential employers, collaborators, or clients to reach out through multiple channels, reducing friction in the hiring or engagement process.

Ultimately, this portfolio will function as my digital business card, resume, and work showcase combined into one cohesive, professional platform that I fully control and can continuously improve.

Target Audience & Scenarios

The primary audience for this portfolio website includes:

  • Hiring Managers and Technical Recruiters: Professionals actively seeking web developers for open positions who need to quickly assess candidate qualifications and work quality.
  • Potential Freelance Clients: Individuals or businesses looking to hire a web developer for specific projects and wanting to evaluate past work and communication style.
  • Fellow Developers and Collaborators: Other professionals in the tech community who may be interested in networking, open-source collaboration, or professional partnerships.
  • Career Mentors and Educators: Instructors or mentors who want to track my progress and provide feedback on my professional development.

Visitor Scenarios

The following questions represent typical inquiries visitors might have when arriving at my portfolio:

"What projects has this developer completed, and what specific technologies and tools did they use to build them?"
"Where can I review this developer's professional background, including their work experience, education, and the technical skills they have acquired?"
"What is the best way to get in touch with this developer, whether through email, a contact form, or professional networking platforms like LinkedIn and GitHub?"

Color Schema

The color palette for this portfolio has been carefully selected to convey professionalism, trustworthiness, and technical expertise while maintaining visual appeal and readability.

Primary Blue #2563EB

Used for headings, navigation elements, buttons, and key interactive components. This professional blue conveys trust and competence.

Secondary Green #10B981

Applied to links, accent highlights, success states, and call-to-action elements. The green adds energy and indicates positive actions.

Neutral Light #F8FAFC

Used for section backgrounds, cards, and to create visual separation between content areas without harsh contrast.

Additional supporting colors include #1E293B for primary text and #64748B for secondary/muted text, ensuring excellent readability across all devices.

Typography

The typography choices for this portfolio prioritize readability, professionalism, and modern aesthetic appeal. Two complementary fonts have been selected:

Poppins (Headings)

The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog

Usage: All headings (h1 through h6), the site logo, navigation links, and section titles. Poppins is a geometric sans-serif with friendly rounded letterforms that feel contemporary and approachable while remaining highly legible at various sizes.

Fallbacks: 'Poppins', system-ui, sans-serif

Inter (Body Text)

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This sentence contains every letter of the alphabet and demonstrates how body text will appear throughout the portfolio website.

Usage: All paragraph text, list items, form labels, and general content. Inter was specifically designed for computer screens with excellent legibility at small sizes, making it ideal for extended reading on web pages.

Fallbacks: 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif

The combination of Poppins for headings and Inter for body text creates a clear visual hierarchy while maintaining a cohesive, professional appearance throughout the site.
